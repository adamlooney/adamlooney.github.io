I am Director of the Marriner S. Eccles Institute for Economics and Quantitative Analysis at the University of Utah, a clinical professor in the Division of Quantitative Analysis of Markets and Organizations, and a Visiting Senior Fellow at the Brookings Institution. My research examines the effects of government programs, with a particular focus on tax and student loan policy.
